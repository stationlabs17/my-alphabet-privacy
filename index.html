My Alphabet – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

My Alphabet is a spelling game for young children. We built it to be safe: it collects no personal information from anyone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. It doesn't access the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

What stays on the device

The app remembers a few game preferences (sound on or off, difficulty, hints, the selected category) and which categories have been unlocked. This is stored only on the device, using standard iOS storage, and is never sent anywhere.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive payment details. Every purchase screen is protected by a parental gate.

Network

All content is built into the app, so it works fully offline. The only network communication is with Apple's App Store, to process and restore purchases.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Because no personal information is collected, nothing is shared with or sold to third parties. The app contains no external links or social features.
